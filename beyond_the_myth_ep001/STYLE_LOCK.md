## 3. Structural Rules & Screenprint Mechanics

1. **Off-Register Color Blocks**: Fills sit slightly offset from their black ink outlines (the classic 1950s screenprint / risograph hallmark).
2. **Theatrical Spotlight**: A geometric diagonal beam of warm translucent light (`polygon` with multiply blend mode) tracks key evidence actions across scenes.
3. **Quirky Institutional Geometry**: The Federal Reserve building, the President, the Senate, and the FOMC table are rendered with charming mid-century perspective, whimsical columns, and angular stylized silhouettes.
4. **Authentic Paper Texture**: An organic grain and subtle dust texture overlay the composition, making every frame feel like an authentic 1960 animated documentary short.
5. **Legibility & Safe Area**: Subtitles are set in crisp mid-century styled title cards with high-contrast text and warm paper badges strictly within `x: 90–990`, `y: 160–1540`.
